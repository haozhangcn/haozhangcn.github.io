I develop intelligent methods for understanding electromagnetic signals, with a focus on spectrum intelligence, wireless communications, and foundation models.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Digital Future Institute (DFI), Khalifa University, working with Prof. Mérouane Debbah.

I received my Ph.D. degree from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics in June 2025. In 2024, I was a visiting Ph.D. student at the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, under the supervision of Prof. Chau Yuen (IEEE Fellow). Before that, I received my M.Eng. and B.S. degrees from Nanchang University in 2020 and 2017.

My research centers on the theory and methods of intelligent electromagnetic signal processing. I have published more than 30 papers in leading journals and conferences, including IEEE COMST, IEEE JSAC, IEEE TIFS, IEEE TCOM, and IEEE TCCN. According to Google Scholar, My publications include two ESI Highly Cited Papers and one cover story. I have also filed or been granted four invention patents and contributed to one international standard.

I received the 2025 Excellent Doctoral Dissertation Award of the China Education Society of Electronics (CESE) and was selected for NUAA’s Top-notch Postgraduate Innovative Talents Training “Yinhang Project.” I regularly review for IEEE JSAC, IEEE TWC, IEEE TCOM, IEEE WCM, IEEE TNNLS, IEEE JSTARS, IEEE GRSM, IEEE CIM, ACM MM, and other venues, and have served as a TPC member for IEEE ICC, IEEE CITIC, and other international conferences.
